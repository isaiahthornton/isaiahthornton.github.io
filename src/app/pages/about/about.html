About me

From Hightstown to shipping my own product

I grew up in Hightstown, New Jersey, and I've been drawn to technology for as long as I can remember. That curiosity took me to Rutgers, where I earned a B.A. in Information Technology and Informatics with two minors. Critical Intelligence Studies trained me to think like an analyst: weigh sources, spot threats, and reason about risk. Digital Communication, Information, and Media taught me something code alone doesn't: how people actually experience what you put in front of them.

After graduating, I stopped treating tech as a subject and started treating it as a craft. I founded ObsidianTrading, a trading journal that's live in beta, and taught myself most of what it took to ship it: authentication, payments, database security, a broker API integration, and the compliance work that came with it. Now I'm strengthening my fundamentals in Revature's software development program, working in C#, ASP.NET Core, Entity Framework Core, SQL Server, and Angular.

I'm early in my career, and I'm upfront about that. What I bring is follow-through. Before moving into tech full-time, I spent years leading teams as a head lifeguard and supervisor, where I learned to train people, stay steady under pressure, and own the outcome. I bring the same mindset to a codebase: pay attention, take ownership, and see it through.
